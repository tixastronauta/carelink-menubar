Your data stays with you

Built for people who care about their health data as much as we do.

🔒 Official sign-in

You sign in on Medtronic's own CareLink page. The app never sees or stores your password.

🔑 Keychain only

Your session is kept in the macOS Keychain on your Mac, not in files.

🚫 No tracking

No analytics, no ads and no servers of ours. The app talks only to CareLink.

👁️ Read-only by design

It can only read what CareLink already shows you. It cannot change your therapy or your pump.

Install in three steps

It takes about a minute. The extra step on first launch is normal for free apps that are not distributed through Apple.

Download and drag

Open CareLink-Menubar.dmg and drag the app onto the Applications folder.

Open it once

Open CareLink Menubar from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.

Allow it

Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Done: the app appears in your menu bar.

Why the warning? Apple charges developers a yearly fee to "notarize" apps. CareLink Menubar is free and its code is public, so you can see exactly what it does. You only approve it once for each new version.

Questions

Short answers to what people usually ask.

Is this an official Medtronic app?

No. CareLink Menubar is an independent, unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medtronic. It reads the same data you can see in CareLink.

Can it change my pump or my insulin?

No. It is read-only by design: it never sends commands to your pump and never changes your therapy.

Does it replace my pump or phone alarms?

No. Keep relying on your pump, sensor and the MiniMed app and their alarms. Always confirm values in the official app before making treatment decisions.

Which accounts and pumps work?

It works with European CareLink accounts (as a patient, or as a care partner with one linked patient). It was developed and tested with a MiniMed Flex. Other pumps and regions may work but haven't been verified yet.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later (Apple Silicon or Intel) and your CareLink login.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac. On first launch you're asked for consent. Readings for the widget are stored locally, and you can withdraw consent at any time to delete them and sign out.
